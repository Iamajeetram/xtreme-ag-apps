Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 27, 2026

Information we collect

Xtreme AG Apps maintains this website to provide information about our products and Google Play closed testing programs. We do not collect personal information through forms on this website. We do not run server-side email collection or store tester email addresses on this site.

Information used for support

If you contact us using the email address provided on the site (support@xtremeagapps.in), your message and the information you send in that email will be used to respond to your request. Emails sent to that address are delivered to our support mailbox; they are not collected via this website or stored in any website database. By emailing us, you are providing your contact information directly to Xtreme AG Apps for support purposes.

Google Play and third-party services

Our apps are distributed and tested using Google Play's closed testing features. Google Play, its testing systems and other third-party services (for example analytics providers used inside our apps) operate under their own privacy policies. If you join a closed test on Google Play, Google may process information about your Google account in accordance with Google's policies. We recommend reviewing Google's privacy documentation for details about how Google handles account and testing data.

Cookies and website data

This website uses minimal, necessary browser features for display and navigation. We do not use tracking cookies or analytics on this site. Browser cookies may be used by your browser for its own functionality, but the site does not set tracking cookies or collect analytics data.

Children's privacy

Our website and closed testing programs are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 via this website. If you believe we have received information about a child under 13, please contact us at support@xtremeagapps.in so we can investigate and delete the information as appropriate.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect information you provide to us (for example via email). However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will post the updated policy on this page with an updated Effective Date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@xtremeagapps.in.
