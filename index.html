How to Join the Test

  1. Send us your Google account email.
  2. Wait until your email is added to the closed testing program.
  3. Open the testing link above using the same Google account and tap "Join the test."

Important: Your Google account email must be added to the tester list before you can join the test. Use the same Google account that was added to the tester list.

If you see a message that the app is unavailable or you are not eligible to test, make sure your Google account has been added to the tester list.
